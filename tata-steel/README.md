# Tata Steel | Financial Analysis & Valuation

## Project Overview

A financial analysis and valuation project on Tata Steel to assess its financial performance, operating trends and valuation using historical financials and forward-looking estimates.

## Objective

To evaluate Tata Steel's financial health and estimate its value using financial statement analysis, forecasting, DCF valuation and comparable-company analysis.

## Financial Analysis

Reviewed historical financial statements to assess revenue, EBITDA, profitability, earnings and cash flow trends.

Key findings included:

- Revenue recovered in FY26 after declining through FY25.
- EBITDA increased by 35.8% in FY26, indicating a strong recovery in operating performance.
- PAT recovered to ₹10,886 Cr in FY26.
- Operating cash flow increased to ₹35,064 Cr in FY26.
- Cash generation remained strong despite significant capital expenditure and financing outflows.

## Peer Comparison

Compared Tata Steel with JSW Steel, SAIL, Jindal Steel & Power and Jindal Stainless across revenue, EBITDA margins, valuation multiples and leverage.

Tata Steel had the highest revenue and absolute EBITDA among the selected peers, while trading at the lowest EV/EBITDA and EV/Revenue multiples.

## Financial Forecasting

Developed financial projections for FY27F–FY31F covering revenue, EBITDA, EBIT, PAT and cash flows.

The forecasts indicate that earnings are expected to grow faster than revenue, suggesting improving operating leverage and cost normalisation.

## Valuation

Performed:

- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation
- Comparable-company valuation

The valuation analysis was used to assess Tata Steel's estimated value relative to its market reference.

## Key Takeaways

The analysis indicated a recovery in Tata Steel's earnings and cash generation, while highlighting the importance of margin improvement, capital allocation, steel-price cyclicality and execution of planned capacity expansion.
